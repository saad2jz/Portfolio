Product Approach
The 4 dimensions of Product Ownership
01 — Discovery
Map the problem before the solution
Understand the user, the business constraints, and the data — audit everything before writing a single user story.
02 — Prioritization
A roadmap that says no as much as yes
Rank backlog items by business impact and delivery cost, not by who asks loudest — and defend that ranking to stakeholders.
03 — Cross-Functional Delivery
Ship with the people who build it
Work daily with engineering, ops, and marketplace partners to turn a spec into something live — security and compliance built in, not bolted on.
04 — Measurement & Iteration
Ship, measure, adjust
Track the one KPI that matters, kill what doesn't move it, and feed the result back into the next sprint.

Zero-Touch Operational Cluster — FFA
End-to-end automation linking Shopify Plus (storefront), Erplain (B2B ERP), Colissimo (logistics), and Pennylane (invoicing). Every order automatically triggers stock updates, a shipping label, and a compliant invoice. Result: 0 manual entries on the primary fulfillment flow, 40% less B2B admin overhead.
Shopify Plusn8nErplain APIPennylaneColissimoREST Webhooks

The problem

FFA's fulfillment process ran on manual entry at every handoff — Shopify orders copied into Erplain, shipping labels made by hand, invoices typed one by one in Pennylane. Every step added lag, human error, and a compliance risk for B2B invoicing.

The approach

  • Mapped the entire fulfillment flow end-to-end before writing a single automation rule
  • Designed a webhook-driven architecture in n8n — each Shopify order event triggers a sequential chain with error handling at every node
  • Built field-level schema mapping between Shopify, Erplain, Colissimo and Pennylane — nothing left unmapped, no silent failures
  • Added server-side validation to catch malformed payloads before they hit downstream systems

The result

0 manual entries −40% admin overhead 100% FinOps compliant Tax-audit ready

Automated lead generation for complex B2B sales cycles. Multi-source scraping (LinkedIn API, Google Maps, Apollo), automated scoring, and personalised multi-channel outbound sequences via Brevo, synced directly into HubSpot CRM.
PythonApifyLinkedIn APIHubSpotBrevon8nCold Email

The problem

Manual B2B prospecting at scale is slow and inconsistent — leads sourced from scattered places, no unified scoring, outreach written one email at a time. The goal: a system that could find, qualify, and contact verified B2B targets with no manual bottleneck.

The approach

  • Multi-source extraction: LinkedIn Sales Navigator via Apify, Google Maps API for local businesses, Apollo for verified email data
  • Automated enrichment — each lead scored on firmographic criteria (company size, sector, decision-maker title) before entering the sequence
  • Personalised outbound sequences built in Brevo, triggered by n8n based on lead-score thresholds
  • Full CRM sync to HubSpot — every touchpoint logged, deal stage updated automatically on reply or click
